# Research synthesis and adopted skills

## Judgement for this brief

The best fit is a layered system: reference-specific editorial direction; explicit issue palette/type choices; quiet, accessible journal navigation; truthful English copy; and independent engineering quality guidance. The broad [catalog](CATALOG.md) is the discovery result, not a request to load or install every skill. It includes more than 50 distinct licensed skills verified against actual pinned entrypoints; [source verification](source-verification.json) records the exact checked count.

“Japanese authenticity” here means a defensible connection to the supplied visual material and awareness of different traditions and eras. A skill name, Japanese vocabulary, repository stars, or distressed texture cannot authenticate a scan or certify cultural authority. We found real specialist skills; their usefulness differs sharply from the name alone.

Our interpretation of the shared principle is relational placement: empty space and density both serve emphasis. A sparse recorder ad, a saturated watch scene and dense theatrical print can each have deliberate hierarchy. This is a connection we infer from observed compositions; it is not an archival statement or a claim that all references embody Wabi-Sabi.

## Limits

Repository stars are evidence of repository attention only. The fit decisions above are our judgement from source instructions, compatibility and the confirmed brief. We have not benchmarked every candidate, verified every maintainer's claim, authenticated the images or rendered an application. [Validation](VALIDATION.md) records what was actually exercised; [authoring](AUTHORING.md) describes the official guidance applied.
