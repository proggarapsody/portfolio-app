See [source verification](source-verification.json) for the independently checked total.

Count real SKILL.md entrypoints, not their individual rule files, theme references, hooks or install-directory mirrors. The top-level Maestro index, a declared derivative, uncertain-original subskills, and files without clear licenses are excluded from the strict count. A declared MIT license without a full upstream text is reported distinctly. Public access does not imply an open-source license.

Repository stars are a retrieval-time snapshot, not per-skill ratings. No reliable comparative skill rating was established. The fit labels are our brief-specific judgement from the inspected instructions and prerequisites, not benchmark scores. The [synthesis](SYNTHESIS.md) explains the strongest choices and specific exclusions.
