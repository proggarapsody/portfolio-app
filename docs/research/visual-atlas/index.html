Five different ways to compose

The diagrams isolate image and type masses. They do not reproduce the artwork, identify typefaces or authenticate a printing process.

06 / Roland

The image is the space.

Visible evidence

The receding instrument planes and dense midtones do the work. Identity and an orange caption sit at the edges.

Design consequence

Choose imagery with depth and a usable quiet area. A flat green surface cannot replace the scene.

02 / Seiko

The object inhabits a world.

Visible evidence

An oversized bright watch, wave environment and smaller pair create three scales within one blue field.

Design consequence

A new montage needs a subject-related idea and believable or deliberately graphic relationships—not just a blue background.

08 / Cinema sheet

Figure and type interlock.

Visible evidence

The red title, narrow columns, portrait mass and bottom black blocks compete within a clear border.

Design consequence

Keep meaningful density. Adapt column and headline relationships into readable English. The supplied date is unverified.

09 / Canon Demi

Two close views create an encounter.

Visible evidence

Eyes above a hand and camera; one enclosing rounded frame; compact type and information below.

Design consequence

Pair related views with purposeful scale. The rounded frame belongs to the whole image, not a repeated card system.

10 / AKIRA

Unequal blocks make a news sheet.

Visible evidence

Headline slab → illustrated event → dense explanation → heavy closing block, with an independent side rail.

Design consequence

Compose these masses before adding material effects. A big title and ordinary hero rectangle lose the structure.

Book & album structure

Proposed adaptations for an English journal. The supplied ads do not document complete books or album packages. Transfer pacing, alignment, facing relationships and real credits; avoid decorative Japanese or a mandatory page-turn simulation.

01 / Announcement to index

Change the density and task; carry a small identity cue forward.

02 / Sleeve to liner notes

The gutter separates visual identity from useful editorial information.

03 / Essay and evidence

Relate image, caption and argument through a consistent page frame.

04 / Reflow, not miniaturization

Preserve English source order and readable type when the spread collapses.

Material lab: one cause at a time

Original graphic specimens below make individual mechanisms visible. No treatment touches the reference scans. Sliders are deliberate demonstrations; their values are not approved design tokens. Resetting restores the starting experiments.

Paper variation

Quiet variation; keep the substrate separate from photographs and reading content.

Ink edge

Inspect the glyph edges at actual size. Stronger wobble is a failure, not extra authenticity.

Two ink plates

Separate authored plates; this is not an RGB shadow on every word.

Graphic dot screen

A flat graphic screen, not a photo-to-halftone conversion. True tonal halftone needs tone-dependent dot coverage.
