# Evidence from the eight supplied images

This is a retrieval record, not a design proposal. All eight attachments were inspected visually with `view_image` at their supplied resolution. Pixel measurements used Pillow only for analysis; no source image was edited. The eight copies in `docs/references/` match their corresponding attachment SHA-256 hashes exactly. `image-palettes.json` records paths, dimensions, hashes, global color bins, localized regions and filtering thresholds. No external archive, publication record, scan metadata or historical date was supplied with these images.

Typography descriptions below identify observable roles and broad letterform families, not exact font names. Japanese snippets are deliberately short. High confidence means the displayed characters are legible; it does not establish publication provenance or the truth of advertising claims. Paragraphs of fine print were not transcribed.

## 1 — Sony recorder advertisement

**Observable composition:** Portrait page. A large SONY wordmark anchors the upper left; a three-line headline sits below with a horizontal rule extending to the right. A grayscale hand and wrist hold a recorder on a strong rising diagonal through the center. The hand establishes scale; the recorder is the largest object. Bottom copy uses narrow columns, bullets and a boxed product lockup with a price. The photography crosses into the bottom text area rather than occupying a separate rectangular picture box.

**Type, color and surface:** Brand serif wordmark, expressive Japanese headline, italic Latin `for PLAY`, small dense Japanese body. Peach/beige paper with uneven yellowing, grain and darker edge staining; charcoal ink; grayscale metallic product and skin. Observed samples: paper `#EED2BD`, logo stroke `#343233`. These describe the supplied scan, not original paper or inks.

**Readable wording:** `サウンド・ハンティング` — “Sound hunting”; `なんでも、かんでも録音しちゃおう` — “Let's record absolutely anything”; `マイクのいらない録音機` — “A recorder that doesn't need a microphone”; `新発売` — “Newly released.” All high confidence; the microphone statement is advertising copy, not a technical conclusion about the device. `Memo` and `¥25,300` are visible. A tiny footer appears to print `© 1970 SONY` (medium confidence at supplied resolution). That is a printed date claim; it does not authenticate the scan or date the physical reproduction.
