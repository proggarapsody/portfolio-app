# Confirmed portfolio brief

Confirmed on 11 October 2026. This preparation task delivers skills and documentation only. No prototypes or application implementation are included.

## Purpose and audience

A central home for the author's creative practice, stories, and software engineering career. Serve employers receiving job applications and visitors interested in photography, music, or writing. Both audiences matter. The professional section must be reachable directly without opening the journal first.

## Editorial experience

The main address presents the current issue's cover: a personally curated photograph, quote, masthead, and selected typography. Opening the cover leads to a contents page with image-led introductions to four sections. Visitors can select a section or continue through an explicit reading sequence.

The experience sits between a physical publication and an ordinary website. Use editorial page composition and restrained transitions. A literal page curl and hover previews are not requirements. All navigation remains available by touch and keyboard.

## Four sections

- **Software engineering:** selected projects, professional experience, GitHub, LinkedIn, CV/contact access, and shareable portfolio/project URLs. Claims must be supported by the author's supplied material.
- **Photography:** albums or photo essays, captions, and original photographic rendering. Do not add retro filters to the work.
- **Music:** the author's published works, artwork, listening controls, and associated notes. Music is a main editorial section; an interactive instrument is outside the initial scope.
- **Writing and stories:** articles, personal stories, and other editorial writing.

## Issues and content

Individual content has permanent URLs. An issue curates selections of that content with its own cover and contents page. Preserve earlier covers and selections in an issue archive. Updating the current issue does not replace older issues or change article URLs.

A weekly or monthly update is possible, but no fixed publishing schedule is required. Agents will assist publishing; existing content will be supplied. A visual CMS is not required for launch.

## Visual and interaction decisions

English only at launch. Newspaper and vintage advertisement references inform layouts, typography, print effects, colors, and concise editorial wording. They do not require Japanese decorative copy or a synthesizer interface.

Maintain a recognizable masthead, navigation, typography system, and section identities across issues. Cover composition, selected photograph, and accent color may vary. Keep the publication name undecided until supplied.

On mobile, keep cover and contents as distinct steps, then allow vertical reading within sections with previous/next navigation. Wider desktop layouts may resemble editorial spreads without imposing tiny columns on phones.

Support visible keyboard focus, touch, reduced motion, readable contrast, and semantic content. Do not hide necessary information behind hover.

## Scope and platform

Next.js on Vercel; an existing domain will be connected later. Start with section browsing, simple tags, issue archives, and contact links. Search, comments, a newsletter, a contact form, visitor analytics, and automated publishing jobs are deferred unless requested later.

Use a small coherent set of project-local skills. A custom skill may capture the supplied art direction. Do not install a broad skill library or start feature work during preparation.
